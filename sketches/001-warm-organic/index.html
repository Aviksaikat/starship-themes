Contiguous Powerline separators (flame , angled , block ), Nerd Font glyphs, no rounded caps, no inter-segment gaps.

Mossfire rationale. The background is #1a2018 — a very dark forest-floor green, distinct from the near-black neutrals that every other Gruvbox-derivative uses. The path chip uses a mid-weight moss green (#7a9e5a); the git chip shifts to ember orange (#c97b36), creating a fire/wood contrast that reads as "forest at dusk" rather than "Halloween palette". The lang chip picks up a warm amber (#d4a853) — turmeric, not gold, avoiding direct Gruvbox Yellow overlap. All three chip hues are desaturated enough to feel botanical rather than garish, yet vivid enough to remain readable on all four production terminal backgrounds (#282a36, #1e1e2e, #24273a, #2e3440). The ❯ uses the brightest sage in the family so the prompt cursor is unambiguous. Segments are joined by the flame separator (U+E0C0 ): the separator glyph's foreground is the preceding segment's background colour, its background is the following segment's background colour — producing a seamless, gap-free transition. The tail flame after the last segment has only a foreground colour (the last chip's bg), landing directly on the terminal background.

Saffron Grove rationale. The background #1e1610 reads as dried bark or dark leather — it has warmth even at near-black luminance, and is meaningfully different from any existing theme in the repo. The path chip uses saffron/turmeric (#c8973a): a yellow-orange mid-point chosen so it doesn't clash with the clay git chip (#b55840). Those two are kept far apart in hue (≈40° apart) so dirty-repo state is unambiguous at a glance. The lang chip breaks the warm tones with sage green (#7ea85f) — the complementary botanical accent that prevents the palette from reading as just "orange stuff on brown". Compared to Gruvbox: Gruvbox uses a grey-brown neutral at #282828 and its yellow is a sharp #d79921; Saffron Grove's bg is genuinely warm-toned and the path chip is deeper and more golden, not lemon. Segments are joined by the angled-cut separator (U+E0BC ) — a sharp diagonal slice that suits the spice-market aesthetic. The separator's fg equals the left segment bg; its bg equals the right segment bg; the tail separator has fg only, dissolving into the terminal background.

Copper Bloom rationale. The background #161c1c is a cold-dark slate with the faintest teal cast — it evokes the shaded side of a copper tank, not a forest or a spice market. The path chip uses verdigris (#4a9e8a): the distinctive blue-green of oxidized copper that immediately distinguishes this palette from anything warm-only. The git chip shifts to raw copper (#b87d4a): the oxide-free metal, sitting at ~160° away from the verdigris on the hue wheel, ensuring strong contrast between path and git state. The lang chip uses a paler patina blue (#7ab8c0), lighter than the path chip, providing a three-level brightness gradient across the prompt. The result reads as "industrial botanical" or "Victorian greenhouse" rather than forest or kitchen — meaningfully different from both Mossfire and Saffron Grove, and unlike any existing theme in the repo. Segments are joined by the block/Lego separator (U+E0CE ) — a rectangular interlocking motif that suits the industrial-copper aesthetic. Same fg=prev-bg / bg=next-bg rule; tail block has fg only.
